Prepare for your Interview

Follow these steps to ensure your audio and video sensors are calibrated properly.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge and open this application through http://127.0.0.1:5000.
  2. Select Allow when the browser asks for camera and microphone permissions.
  3. Check the camera preview and microphone status on the next page.
  4. Monitoring starts automatically after camera and microphone permissions are granted. If a device disconnects, reconnect it before continuing.
  5. Speak clearly. You can finish each answer manually or allow the silence timer to stop listening.
